Selected work

01

Automated RF gasket cutting machine

Applied Materials

assets/gasket-01.jpg

RF gaskets were being cut by hand against a tape measure — slow, and the tolerance depended on who was doing it. I built a machine that feeds stock to a set length and signals the operator when to cut. Rotary encoders track real fed length and stop the feed at target. I designed the mechanics, wired the panel, wrote the firmware, and built the operator interface.

Raspberry Pi 5Arduino MegaNEMA 23 stepper Rotary encodersPython / TkinterArduino C++
02

O-ring cleaning station

Applied Materials

assets/oring-01.jpg

A benchtop station that washes and dries O-rings on a repeatable cycle instead of by hand. Built on DIN-rail industrial components so it could live on the floor and be serviced by the people using it — deionized water through an inline filter for the wash, DI air knife for the dry.
