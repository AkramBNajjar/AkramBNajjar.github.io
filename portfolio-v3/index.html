Automated RF Gasket Cutting Machine

RF gaskets were cut by hand against a tape measure, so accuracy depended on who was cutting. This machine feeds stock to a set length and tells the operator when to cut.

A Raspberry Pi 5 runs the touchscreen and supervises an Arduino Mega real-time motion controller. Closed-loop stepper positioning uses rotary encoder feedback, so it stops on true fed length rather than step count. I also specified the full power distribution and terminal block wiring. Automated O-Ring Cleaning Machine

A portable machine that washes and dries O-rings on a repeatable cycle instead of by hand. An Arduino-based PLC programmed in C++ sequences the cycle, driving stepper motion through the wash and dry stages and switching pumps and air through relay modules.

Deionized water runs through an inline filter for the wash, and a DI air knife handles the dry. I produced the production drawings and BOMs in Siemens NX with Teamcenter.

F1TENTH ROS 2, Python

F1TENTH Path-Tracking Study

I implemented pure pursuit, Stanley, and MPC controllers as ROS 2 nodes, using both kinematic and dynamic bicycle models, then benchmarked cross-track error and lap time across speeds.

The analysis pipeline logs state at 100 Hz and runs automated parameter sweeps. One finding: the lookahead setting with the lowest mean error never finished a lap. The study extends into vehicle dynamics, covering surface friction, understeer and oversteer balance, and load transfer under braking.

Hollow support tubes and a copper heat sink

I redesigned the system's bolted solid support tubes as hollow sections. Because bending stiffness depends mostly on material far from the center, the change cut material use by 20% while keeping bending stiffness, with tolerances set using GD&T. Drag the slider to see why.

I also designed a copper heat sink in SolidWorks to replace an aluminum part. FEA showed a 6% thermal improvement, and I machined the final part myself on a CNC mill.
